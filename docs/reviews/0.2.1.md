# Review of 0.2.0 (for maintenance release 0.2.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-19 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md`, starting from the review notes recorded under 0.2.0. The review covered the four new tax models and their tests, the parameter sources, the seven new fixture cities at desktop and 390 px, and the pay-detail wording.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures. Medium = a test or source gap on money logic, or confusing UX. Low = tidy-up.

## Passed

- All checks green before changes: 97 unit tests, 27 end-to-end checks, type-check, boundaries, data validation.
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px on Tallinn, Porto, Valencia or Berlin; no console errors.
- Size inside budget: release data 65 kB, compiled JS 89 kB (budget 500 kB and 150 kB).
- No dead code; the remaining exported-but-local helpers are used within their own modules.
- CI annotations are Playwright's test summary only; no warnings.

## Findings

| ID | Area | Severity | Finding | Fix | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Tax, Spain | High | The 2026 tax credit for low earners (Real Decreto-ley 5/2026: up to €590.89, full to €17,094 gross, gone at €20,048) was missing, so take-home was too low for gross pay under about €20,000. | Credit added, never more than the tax due; tests at €18,000 and €20,000. | Fixed |
| S2 | Tax, Spain | Medium | A household with one earner and a non-earning partner gave the earner only half of the child allowances, losing the other half. | A sole earner takes the full child allowances (as a one-income couple would by filing jointly); two earners split them. Tests for both. | Fixed |
| S3 | Sources, Spain | Medium | The Madrid and Valencian scales came from a secondary site. | Confirmed against the official texts: Madrid Ley 13/2023 (BOCM, applies from 2023, no later change found) and Valencian Ley 5/2026 article 18 (DOGV, applies from 1 January 2026). Both now high confidence. | Fixed |
| S4 | Tests, Germany | Medium | Germany's net-pay tests were hand-worked from the same parameters as the model. | Added ten points from the published 2026 basic tax table, covering income tax and solidarity surcharge from €20,000 to €132,000. All match exactly. | Fixed |
| S5 | Copy | Low | Pay-detail notes used tax jargon ("child minimums", "special-expense allowance", "income splitting", "regional official bulletin"). | Notes rewritten in plain language for all six countries. | Fixed |
| S6 | UX | Low | The "Married or registered partners" checkbox didn't say what it changes. | Added a hint: it changes tax in Germany and the US. | Fixed |
| S7 | Tests | Low | Official calculators (Spanish tax agency, Portuguese tax portal) can't be reached from the build environment. | Deferred: needs a machine with access. Risk reduced by S3 and S4, and by Portugal's brackets matching the published per-bracket column. | Deferred to backlog |
| S8 | Tax, Germany | Low | Church tax uses Berlin's 9%. Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg use 8%. | Deferred: Berlin is the only German city planned. Make the rate per city if another German city is added. | Deferred to backlog |
| S9 | Docs | Low | `docs/data-update.md` still described Spain's sources as secondary and Germany as untested against tables. | Updated. | Fixed |
