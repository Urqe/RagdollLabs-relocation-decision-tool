# Official calculator cross-checks (review 0.3.1, T6)

One case per pilot country, to run in each country's official calculator in a browser. Each case is a single adult, no children, no church membership, a full year of wage income only, tax year 2026. Send back the calculator's figures; they will be added as reference tests, and any difference above €1 a month (or the local equivalent) becomes a finding.

Notes for comparing:

- Germany: the app models the annual assessment; the monthly payslip calculator can differ slightly because of rounding and the way health insurance is deducted.
- Spain: the AEAT simulator may apply the 2025 low-income work reduction rule, which the app also uses; at €50,000 it does not apply.
- Mexico: payroll calculators withhold monthly; the annual tax can differ from twelve monthly withholdings by a few pesos because of the annual adjustment.
