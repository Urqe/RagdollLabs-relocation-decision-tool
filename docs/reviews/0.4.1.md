# Review of 0.4.0 (for maintenance release 0.4.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md` and the notes recorded under 0.4.0. The review covered the matching core (`src/core/fit/fit.ts`), the preference definition and indicator data and how they are derived, the fit section in Explore, the "What matters to you" form and "How it works", on desktop, 390 px and dark mode.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures. Medium = a test or source gap, or confusing UX. Low = tidy-up.

## Passed

- All checks green before changes: 245 unit tests; 92 end-to-end checks in Chromium on desktop, 390 px and tablet; type-check, boundaries and data validation. The last CI run on `main` (0.4.0) passed, and the GitHub Actions in use are on their latest major versions (checkout v7, setup-node v7, upload-pages-artifact v5, deploy-pages v5).
- The matching core reads no browser globals; hidden aspect choices (for an area left at "Not important") cannot change a result; restored preferences are validated.
- Re-fetched from Eurostat Urban Audit: Helsinki 17.5% and Madrid 26.1% residents born abroad (2024) match exactly.
- The editorial bounds in `data/preferences.json` read fairly for the five pilot cities, apart from V1 below. Checked city by city for every area and for the alternative choices (cold winters, cool or hot summers, getting around by car); Helsinki's low climate score for anyone avoiding dark winters is by design and explained.
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px; no console errors; sizes within budget (see Verification).
