## Helsinki–Sacramento reproduction

Illustrative resident employment scenario: married couple, one earner at €92,400 gross a year, two-bedroom city-wide rent, moderate lifestyle, no children, same gross salary in both cities, ECB snapshot 2026-10-19. Amounts below are rounded euros per month. One car is tested *only in Sacramento*, retaining transit in Helsinki; the global household control cannot express this split in a single comparison.

| Assumption | Helsinki estimated savings | Sacramento estimated savings | Reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| No car in either city; employer family plan in Sacramento | €1,420 | €1,893 | Sacramento leads by about €473. Its rent is an unverified metro HUD figure. |
| No car in Helsinki; one new small sedan in Sacramento | €1,420 | €1,361 | Helsinki leads by about €59. Sacramento transport changes from $200 in passes to $798 for one car and one pass. |

The 0.7.0 calculation used $512/month for two people's healthcare; the corrected family-plan scenario uses about $843. A different employer contribution, medical use or a rent above the 40th-percentile metro figure can change either comparison. No feasible job offer, residency permission or pension-savings equivalence is implied by equal gross salaries. A paid-off car costs less than the new-car estimate, and its value should be tested with a personal transport override rather than assumed away.

At €92,400 + €48,000 gross for two earners, corrected Sacramento savings are about €4,586 without cars and €4,053 with one; Helsinki is about €4,415 without cars. The one-car Sacramento case still leads Helsinki's no-car case by about €362, so the reversal is scenario-dependent, not a universal verdict.

## Source and uncertainty checks

- **Canada:** 2026 federal/Ontario rates, Ontario basic personal amount ($12,989), CPP and EI ceilings agree with the [CRA 2026 Ontario payroll tables](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4032-payroll-deductions-tables/t4032on-jan/t4032on-january-general-information.html) and [CRA 2026 rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html). The $80,000 unit case remains an independent published calculator comparison, not a result from CRA's interactive PDOC. PDOC requires interactive inputs and a comparable official output was not obtained; do not claim an official end-to-end calculator match. Ontario's low-income tax reduction and child cash benefits remain excluded as documented in the model. They can matter to lower-income families.
- **US health:** [KFF's 2025 employer survey](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/2025-employer-health-benefits-survey/) reports $1,440/year worker single and $6,850/year worker family premium. The existing approximately $136/person/month out-of-pocket component comes from national 2024 CMS spending, not a family-plan-specific forecast. It does not represent a bad-year deductible or out-of-pocket maximum. Employer-plan eligibility and subsidy are a major unknown; the warning is shown with the result.
- **Sacramento prices:** BEA's 2024 metro parity is [105.168 for goods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RPPGOOD40900) and [100.267 for other services](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RPPSERVEOTH40900) versus US=100. Rent is already a metro figure. A rough 5% goods uplift is about $19 per adult equivalent for the current $385 grocery line, and a negligible other-services uplift on the lifestyle components. This does not by itself repair the car/health reversal and would suggest more precision than the national-to-city basket supports. Keep national-priced rows labelled as such until the directional-city contract. The $191 eating-out category combines restaurants **and hotels**, so a blanket tip surcharge would be a false calculation; even a 20% stress on the whole category is under $40 per adult equivalent. Record tips as a possible extra rather than adding a fabricated exact adjustment.
- **Rent:** Sacramento's FY2027 HUD rents and Austin's FY2026 HUD rents still rely on republications and require direct HUD verification. These 40th-percentile metro rents are not current apartment listings. Use personal rent overrides for an actual shortlist.

## Remaining risk

US healthcare, insurance deductible, individual rent and car need can each exceed the narrow numeric ranges currently shown. Several national-price observations have point values; these are model inputs, **not confidence intervals**. The warnings and the two-city example prevent a known conditional comparison from being presented as robust, but the product still needs the planned directional range contract and per-city scenario controls in 0.9–0.10. Pension equivalence, tax benefits, local tips and work authorization remain outside these estimates. No blanket city-price multiplier was added without a defensible category mapping.
