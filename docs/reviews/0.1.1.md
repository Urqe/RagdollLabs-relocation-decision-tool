# Review of 0.1.0 (for maintenance release 0.1.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-19 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md`: a code read-through, scripted checks (unused exports, bundle size, colour contrast), and screenshots plus scripted UX checks on desktop, at 390 px, in dark mode, at 200% text size and by keyboard.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures, or a control that cannot be used. Medium = an accessibility failure, a test gap on money logic, or confusing UX. Low = tidy-up.

**Status** is updated as each item is fixed or deferred. All items were fixed in 0.1.1; none were deferred.

## Passed

- Unit tests (58) and end-to-end tests (13) pass; type-check and module boundaries pass.
- Size well inside budget: release data 22 kB, compiled JS 67 kB (budget 500 kB and 150 kB).
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px or at 200% text size on a 640 px window.
- Keyboard order is logical; focus rings show on custom radio buttons and selects.
- Text contrast meets 4.5:1 in both themes, except the item noted below.
- No `innerHTML` anywhere; external links use `rel="noopener"`.

## Findings

| ID | Area | Severity | Finding | Fix | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | UX | High | On desktop, pressing Enter on the "Your household" heading collapses the whole form, and a mouse cannot reopen it because the heading ignores clicks there. | Render the panel as a plain section on desktop; keep the collapsible version on phones only. | Fixed: plain section on desktop; collapsible only on phones. |
| R2 | UX | High | In monthly mode, changing "payments per year" keeps the old annual figure and silently rewrites the monthly amount. 4,000 a month switched to 14 payments showed 3,429 a month instead of 4,000 × 14. | Keep the monthly amount the user typed and recalculate the annual figure. Show the annual figure beside the field. | Fixed: monthly amount kept; annual figure shown under the field. |
| R3 | UX | High | The discretionary presets say "€500 per adult", but the amount is per adult equivalent, so children add 30% each. The figure on screen does not match the label. | Label presets as "per adult, children counted at 30%", or show the household total next to the choice. | Fixed: hint shows the household total and how it is sized. |
| R4 | UX | Medium | Clearing the income field, or typing an invalid value, leaves the previous results on screen with no message. | Mark the field invalid (`aria-invalid`, inline message) and show "Enter an income to see results" instead of stale figures. | Fixed: field marked invalid with a message; results replaced by a prompt. |
| R5 | UX | Medium | A link to an unknown city id (`#/explore/does-not-exist`) silently shows the first city. | Show "That city isn't in this data release" with the city picker. | Fixed: "City not found" with the picker. |
| R6 | Accessibility | Medium | Input, select and choice-button borders are 1.4:1 against their background (WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 on control boundaries), in both themes. | Darken the control border token in both themes to at least 3:1. | Fixed: control border 3.65:1 (light), 3.64:1 (dark) against surfaces. |
| R7 | Accessibility | Medium | White labels on the groceries ribbon segment are 4.27:1, below 4.5:1 for small text. | Darken the groceries colour or switch its label to dark text. | Fixed: groceries colour darkened; white label now 4.92:1. |
| R8 | UX | Medium | On phones the household form fills the first screen; the city name starts 760 px down. (Already listed.) | Start collapsed after the first visit, with a one-line summary. | Fixed: collapsed with a one-line summary on returning visits. |
| R9 | Tests | Medium | The Finnish Yle tax is only tested where it is capped at €160; incomes between the €15,150 threshold and the cap are untested. | R12 | UX | Low | The household form is shown on the "How it works" page, where it does nothing. | Hide the panel on that page. | Fixed: panel hidden on "How it works". |
| R13 | UX | Low | Income fields show 50000 with no thousands separator, which is hard to check at a glance. | Show the formatted amount under the field, or use a formatted text input. | Fixed: formatted amount in the field hint. |
| R14 | Copy | Low | "Scaled to 1.0 adult equivalents (modified OECD scale)" is jargon in the main breakdown. | Say "Sized for your household (2 adults, 1 child)" and keep the method on the "How it works" page. | Fixed: "Sized for 2 adults and 1 child." |
| R15 | Code | Low | Dead code: `sumDeductions`, `toEurRange` and `uid` are exported but never used. | Remove. | Fixed: removed. |
| R16 | Code | Low | Band messages are written with the band name and then stripped with a regular expression before display. | Store the message without the name. | Fixed. |
| R17 | Code | Low | The data-release version is built from the exchange-rate date, so a data change without new rates keeps the same version. | Version the data release separately in `data/release.json` and bump it when any data changes. | Fixed: `dataVersion` in data/release.json, validated. |
| R18 | Code | Low | The boundary check reads `import … from` but not `export … from` or dynamic `import()`. | Cover both forms. | Fixed: re-exports and dynamic imports checked. |
