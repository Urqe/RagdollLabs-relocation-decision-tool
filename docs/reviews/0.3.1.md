# Review of 0.3.0 (for maintenance release 0.3.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-19 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md` and the notes recorded under 0.3.0. The review covered the Mexican tax model, the pilot cities' data and how it was made, the lifestyle and price-level derivation, the new own-figure inputs, validation, and the Explore and "How it works" screens on desktop, 390 px, tablet width and dark mode.

This time the statistics sites were reachable from the build environment, so values could be checked against the original sources rather than the copies used in 0.3.0.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures. Medium = a test or source gap on money logic, or confusing UX. Low = tidy-up.

## Passed

- All checks green before changes: 219 unit tests; 86 end-to-end checks in Chromium on desktop, 390 px and tablet; type-check, boundaries and data validation. CI also ran WebKit (desktop and iPad) for the first time and passed.
- 44 curated inputs re-fetched from the original APIs match exactly: Finnish household consumption by purpose, population, household budget survey and household size (Eurostat); HICP for Finland, Germany and Spain; 2021 euro exchange rates for USD and MXN; household electricity prices for Finland, Germany and Spain; out-of-pocket health spending for Finland, Germany, Spain and Mexico; all 25 ICP 2021 price levels (World Bank).
- Helsinki rents read from vuokra-arvio.fi in 0.3.0 match Statistics Finland table 15fa for 2026 Q2 exactly (€21.19 and €20.26 per m²).
- Every pilot-city cost row has a source, a period, a confidence level and an explanation; missing costs show "No data" and can be entered.
- Derived city values match their curated inputs (checked by `npm run validate`).
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px on any pilot city or "How it works"; no console errors.
- Equivalent-household tests pass for all seven models, including Mexico.
