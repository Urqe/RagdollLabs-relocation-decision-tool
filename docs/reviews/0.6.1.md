# Review of 0.6.0 (for maintenance release 0.6.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-21 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md` and the notes recorded under 0.6.0. The review covered the five new cities' data and derivations, Portuguese joint taxation, the city search and grouped pickers, and a regression of the five pilot cities. It also put in place the owner's rule from 2026-10-21: where no official source is at hand, published claims can be used as **unverified estimates**, labelled in the app and checked in a verification release before 1.0.0.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures. Medium = a data or test gap, or confusing UX. Low = tidy-up.

## Passed

- All checks green before changes: 289 unit tests; 184 end-to-end checks in Chromium on desktop, 390 px and tablet; type-check, boundaries and data validation. The 0.6.0 CI run on `main` passed with WebKit and deployed. GitHub Actions are on their latest major versions.
- **Regression of the pilot cities:** 0.6.0 changed only retrieval dates in the five pilot city files; their tax models are untouched. Budgets and fits for Helsinki, Berlin, Madrid, Austin and Hermosillo are unchanged.
- **Estonia, official parameters:** the Tax and Customs Board's 2026 summary confirms 22% income tax, the €700 monthly allowance for everyone, 1.6% unemployment insurance, the 2% funded pension default and the €886 social-tax minimum base. The 0.6.0 note that €886 might be the 2025 minimum wage is resolved: it is the 2026 base. The published calculator case (€2,000 a month → €1,658) still matches.
- **Portugal, joint taxation:** the joint and separate returns reproduce a published worked example to the cent (Doutor Finanças, €20,000 and €50,000 on the 2017 scale: separate €18,234.13, joint €17,433.18). The 2026 brackets agree with a second report of the 2026 budget (idealista, 9 October 2025), and are consistent with the 2025 scale updated by 3.51%. The official AT simulator cannot be run from here; the published example checks the method instead.
- **Households:** one earner plus a partner gives the same result as a second income of zero, and swapping incomes changes nothing, in every model including Portugal's joint returns.
- **City search:** finds by city, country or region, ignores accents, ranks name matches first; Enter, arrow keys and Escape work; the match count is announced. No accessibility problems with results open.
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px; no console errors; colours used for the new labels meet 4.5:1 in both themes.
