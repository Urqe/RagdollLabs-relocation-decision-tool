# Review of 0.5.0 and the whole journey (for maintenance release 0.5.1)

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against the checklist in `RELEASE_PLAN.md` and the notes recorded under 0.5.0. The review covered the ranking core (`src/core/rank/rank.ts`), how a salary per city enters the money result (`src/core/budget/city-result.ts`, `src/state/store.ts`), Find, Compare and their tests, and the journey Explore → Find → Compare on the five pilot cities, on desktop, 390 px and dark mode.

**Severity:** High = wrong or misleading figures. Medium = a test gap, or confusing or misleading UX. Low = tidy-up.

## Passed

- All checks green before changes: 267 unit tests; 137 end-to-end checks in Chromium on desktop, 390 px and tablet; type-check, boundaries and data validation. The last CI run on `main` (0.5.0) passed with WebKit, with no warning annotations. The GitHub Actions in use are on their latest major versions (checkout v7, setup-node v7, upload-pages-artifact v5, deploy-pages v5).
- The ranking core is pure and reads no browser globals. Fixtures never take part; must-haves are applied before ranking; incomplete budgets are never inside a money ranking.
- The journeys tell one story: Explore, Find and Compare use the same fit (`computeFit`), the same must-have check and the same savings in euros (`savings.typical / fxRate`), so a city's fit band, essentials result and savings are the same on all three screens.
- Share links: tested with and without salaries; the household is never in the link, salaries only when ticked, and the address drops them once applied.
- Data: 0.5.0 added no data and changed no tax parameters, so the data and tax checklists had nothing new to cover. Validation passes.
- Contrast of the colours 0.5.0 introduced (band and fit words, the "set for this city" mark, missing-data notes, close-to-call notes) is at least 4.5:1 on their backgrounds in both themes. Nothing relies on colour alone: every band is also a word.
- No horizontal scrolling at 390 px; no console errors on any screen.
